Opening message paste once, with your photos attached


    
    
Which photos to attach

    
    
      
  • Face straight to camera, relaxed, no filter.
    • 
      
  • A big natural smile, and one serious or thoughtful look.
    • 
      
  • Three-quarter turn to the left, and one to the right.
    • 
      
  • One waist-up shot in the outfit you want the site to show.
    • 
      
  • Good daylight, no sunglasses, no heavy beauty-mode edits.
    • 
    

  


  

    
Settings that matter

    
    
      
  • Use Gemini's image creation with the highest-quality image model your Pro plan offers, and ask for 4K every time (the prompts already do).
    • 
      
  • Keep every pose of one scene in the same chat, so the face and the camera distance stay the same.
    • 
      
  • Download the original full-size PNG, not a screenshot and not a shared preview.
    • 
      
  • If the face drifts, re-attach your photos and add: match my reference photos exactly.
    • 
      
  • If the green background shows a floor, shadow or gradient, add: the green must be one flat colour edge to edge, no floor, no shadow.
    • 
      
  • If skin or hair looks green-tinted, add: remove all green spill from skin, hair and clothes.
    • 
    

  


  


  
Not sure about a shot? Skip it. Every scene works with any number of poses from one up, and the scenes you do not send keep their current video frames.
